## Deep act

A deep act is undistracted attention on one of these: framing the problem, making the design decision, reviewing until you can explain the result, writing the claim and the prose, or learning something the agent cannot hold for you.

The agent's turn-taking on that act is cybernetic collaboration. It spends the intensity the deep act needs. Name the deep act. Let the agent execute the part it can mostly do, after that act is decided. Carrying the prompting loop is not the deep act.

## Ceilings

The mission ceiling is three. The project ceiling is three. One personal deep block fills the day.

Work hours carry the job's one project from the inventory. The personal deep block is a different part of the day. The German daily ceiling in the inventory still happens on that day.

When the inventory already holds three missions, a day call says the ceiling is full. It refuses a fourth. It does not ask for a drop to two. Life mode is where the missions change.

## Clear yes

A new commitment is a clear yes, and it names the mission or project that pauses to make room. Schedule a commitment that fits under both ceilings.

When it does not fit, name what would pause and end the call. There is no plan for the surplus. When Tejas overrules that in one sentence, state the cost and end the call the same way.

A mission with an end date in the inventory ends on that date. Life mode is the call that keeps it.
